# Documentary & Long-Form YouTube Services

I provide research-backed script and production handoff packages for long-form YouTube, business stories, documentaries, and narrative channels.

## Basic — $75

- 1,200–1,500 word script
- Hook + chapter structure
- Editor / B-roll notes
- 1 revision
- Best when you already have a clear topic or research brief

## Standard — $175

- 1,800–2,500 word script
- Structured research notes and source links
- Hook, pacing, curiosity/payoff structure
- Editor / B-roll notes
- 2 revisions

## Premium — $350

- 2,000–3,000 word documentary script
- Research/source pack
- Retention structure + editor cues
- Narration-ready audio using an approved preset voice when requested
- Subtitle/SRT handoff
- Technical QA receipt
- 2 revisions

## Working principles

- No fabricated sources
- No copied scripts
- No fake client/channel performance claims
- Voice cloning only when the client owns and explicitly authorizes the reference voice
- Factual claims remain source-traceable
- Commercial voice/music rights must pass the delivery gate

## Project intake

When contacting me, include:

- Topic
- Target audience
- Reference channels
- Target duration
- Tone
- Must-use sources or source material
- Claims that need special verification
- CTA / sponsor requirements
- Narration required: yes/no
- Deadline
